## Things I've Built at Slido

### 📊 [Slido for PowerPoint](https://www.slido.com/powerpoint-polling) · *current focus*
Embed polls, quizzes, surveys and Q&A right into PowerPoint slides and have them launch automatically during the presentation.

Most of my time goes into the **macOS** integration (Swift, C, C++ and yes, even VBA). I also contribute to the **Windows** version (.NET/C#) and its frontend (React/TypeScript).

### 📽️ [Slido for Google Slides](https://www.slido.com/google-slides-polling)
The same idea for Google Slides, built from several moving parts: a Chrome extension, a Google Slides add-on, a custom web player and the Slido Sidebar.

I helped build the Chrome extension and the add-on in the early days, before a dedicated team took the project over.

### 🔀 Slido Switcher · *8 years, retired*
Native Windows and macOS apps that let presenters remote-control their computer from the Slido admin, switching between Slido and their slides mid-presentation. They have since been replaced by the deeper PowerPoint and Google Slides integrations above.

### 💬 Slido for Slack · *retired*
Create and manage Slido events and ask questions without leaving Slack. It had a nice run on Product Hunt but not enough users to survive, so it was shut down after two years. I built the backend (Node.js/TypeScript), the MySQL database and the Slack API integration.

## Off the Clock at MakerSpace

In my free time I volunteer at [MakerSpace Bratislava](https://www.msba.sk), a community workshop at Nová Cvernovka. I help run the space and help people with their electronics and maker projects. I also teach:

- 🔋 **[Electronics fundamentals](https://msba.sk/elektro-dielna/zaklady-elektroniky/)**: my full-day course for complete beginners covering circuits, measurements, components and soldering
- 🤖 **[Electronics club for kids](https://msba.sk/elektro-dielna/elektro-kruzok/)**: weekly sessions where 8–15 year-olds go from blinking LEDs to their own Arduino projects
- 📐 **3D modelling for printing** *(coming soon)*: a weekly course on designing functional parts in Autodesk Fusion
